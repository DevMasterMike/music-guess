Accessibility statement

Updated October 15, 2026.

Our goal

  • We want everyone to be able to play Music Guess, including people who are blind, have low vision, or have motor, cognitive or photosensitive conditions.
  • We design and test against Apple's accessibility guidelines and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, where they apply to a game.

What the game supports

  • VoiceOver: Every button and screen is labeled, and the four answers are read in order.
  • More time: Answer time can be set to 25 seconds, and it starts at 25 if VoiceOver is on when you first open the game.
  • Your own pace: Turn off Auto-advance to move to the next song only when you're ready.
  • Pause anywhere: Pause from the screen, Control Center, the Lock Screen or your headphones.
  • Keyboards: Press 1 to 4 to answer, Return for the next song and Escape to pause.
  • Larger text: Text follows your text size setting, and long pages like this one grow to the largest sizes.
  • Reduce Motion: The spinning record, beat bumps and moving backgrounds stop.
  • Not by color alone: Right and wrong answers also show a check mark or a cross.
  • Contrast: Text meets the WCAG contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1.
  • Haptics: Answers can be felt, and on iOS 27 the beat of each song too. Haptics can be turned off.
  • Touch targets: Buttons are at least 44 by 44 points.

Known limits

  • The game is about recognizing music by ear, so it isn't playable without hearing. Beat haptics give a feel for the rhythm, but not enough to name a song.
  • Voice Control can open every menu, but its speech delay costs time in timed rounds. Answer time of 25 seconds helps.
  • The game uses a dark interface only.

Feedback

  • If something gets in your way, email berinshteyn@gmail.com, and we will reply within 5 business days.
  • Please tell us your device, iOS version and the accessibility features you use.
