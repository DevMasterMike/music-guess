Common questions

  • Do I need an Apple Music subscription? No. Rounds use the short previews Apple Music offers to everyone. You only need to allow access to Apple Music.
  • Why does it ask for Apple Music access? To find songs for each deck, and your recently played songs for the Your Music deck. Nothing leaves your device.
  • The game says it can't load songs. Check your internet connection, and that Media & Apple Music access is on in Settings, under Privacy & Security.
  • Is the Daily the same for everyone? Yes, the same ten songs, from Apple Music's daily global chart. Your first try each day counts.
  • How do I play with friends? Duel puts two players on one screen. Online plays with up to six people through Game Center.
  • Can I hear explicit songs? Players 18 and over can turn on Explicit songs in Settings. The Daily and online games always stay clean.
  • How do I reset my stats? In the game, open Settings and choose Reset stats.
  • Does my progress sync? Yes, through your own iCloud account when iCloud is on.
  • I need more time to answer. In Settings, set Answer time to 25 seconds and turn off Auto-advance.
