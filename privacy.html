Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026.

The short version

  • Music Guess does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking.
  • The app reads your Apple Music on your device to build games, and never sends it to us.
  • Your stats stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.
  • Apple runs the services the game uses, such as Apple Music, Game Center and iCloud, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Who we are

  • Music Guess is made by Michael Berinshteyn, an independent developer in Erie, Pennsylvania, United States ("we", "us").
  • Questions about privacy go to berinshteyn@gmail.com.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or advertising identifier.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • The app does not send any data to servers we run, and we do not run any.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Apple Music

  • With your permission, the app uses Apple's MusicKit to read the Apple Music catalog.
  • For the Your Music deck, it also reads your recently played songs, most played songs and playlists.
  • The Deck Store shows charts, playlists and artists on Apple Music. When you search it, what you type goes to Apple Music to find results, like any Apple Music search. We never see it.
  • This happens on your device. Your library and listening history are used only to pick songs for your games.
  • We never receive, store or share your library or listening history.
  • If you have a subscription and tap the plus button next to a song, the app adds it to your Apple Music library. It never changes your library otherwise.
  • Songs play as the short previews Apple Music provides. They stream from Apple, are held in memory only while you play, and are never saved to your device.
  • On iOS 27 and later, the app finds each song's beat on your device with Apple's Music Understanding framework, to time haptics. No audio or results leave your device.
  • An Apple Music subscription is not required.
  • You can turn off access at any time in your device's Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Media & Apple Music. The game needs this access to play.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, stats, level, Daily results and streaks are stored on your device.
  • Daily reminder: If you turn it on, the reminders are scheduled on your device. No data is sent anywhere to deliver them, and you can turn them off in the game or in your device's Settings.
  • The Home Screen widget reads your latest Daily result from storage shared only with the widget, on the same device.
  • Nothing is collected while you play, apart from what you see in Stats.

Apple services the app uses

  • iCloud: If iCloud is on, your stats and Daily results sync between your devices through your own iCloud account. We cannot see or access them.
  • Game Center: If you sign in, your scores and achievements go to Apple and may be shown on leaderboards and to friends, based on your Game Center settings.
  • Online games: Other players see your Game Center nickname. During a game, your answers and answer times go to their devices through Game Center. There is no chat, and we do not receive or keep any of it.
  • Nearby: If you choose Play nearby, the game asks for Local Network access and connects directly to other players' devices near you over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity. Your nickname (or your device's name) and the same game messages go only to those devices, encrypted. We do not receive or keep any of it, and nothing is shared until you open Nearby.
  • SharePlay: If you play over FaceTime, the same game messages go to the other people in the call through SharePlay, which Apple runs. We do not receive or keep any of it.
  • Blend (optional): Blend is off until you turn on "Share my songs for Blend" in an online lobby, and the switch applies to that game only. When a Blend starts, up to 15 songs from your recently played and most played lists (title, artist, album and Apple Music links) go to the player hosting the game, who mixes everyone's songs into rounds. The other players see a song only when it's played, with your name beside it. Nothing is kept after the game, and we never receive any of it.
  • Party codes: If you host a party, you choose who to share its code with.
  • Age range: Where the law requires it, the app asks Apple for an age range, such as "18 or over". Your birth date is never shared with us. The answer is used only to keep explicit songs off for players under 18, and it is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Crash reports: If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. We use them only to fix bugs.
  • App Store: Apple gives developers anonymous, aggregate reports, such as total downloads. These do not identify you.
  • Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy at apple.com/privacy.

What you choose to share

  • When you share a result, you choose where it goes using your device's share sheet.
  • Shared results show your score and how each song went, as a picture and a line of squares, never song names.
  • Links to songs open on Apple Music, which is run by Apple.
  • We do not receive a copy of anything you share.

Third parties

  • The only outside services the app uses are Apple's, listed above.
  • Apple protects that data under Apple's Privacy Policy, which offers at least the protection described here.

Children

  • Music Guess is for a general audience.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, or under 16 in some countries.
  • Because the app collects no personal information, it does not need parental consent under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • Parents can manage Apple Music, Game Center and explicit content for their children with Family Sharing and Screen Time.
  • Parents or guardians with questions can email berinshteyn@gmail.com.

Your privacy rights

  • Laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act and other U.S. state privacy laws give you rights over your personal data.
  • Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete, transfer or opt you out of.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling.
  • You can still contact us at berinshteyn@gmail.com with any request, and we will reply within 30 days.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • We keep no data about you, because we receive none.
  • Your stats stay on your device until you reset them, delete everything, or delete the app.
  • To erase them, open Settings in the game and choose Reset stats (stats and Daily history) or Delete everything (also your settings and recent decks, on this device and in your iCloud copy), or delete the app.
  • To remove synced stats from iCloud, open your device Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, and manage the storage for Music Guess.
  • To stop the app reading Apple Music, turn off its access under Privacy & Security, then Media & Apple Music.
  • To manage Game Center data, use the Game Center settings on your device.

Security

  • Data on your device is protected by your device's built-in security.
  • iCloud, Apple Music and Game Center data are protected by Apple.

Changes to this policy

  • If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top.
  • If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the app's update notes.
